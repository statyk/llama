## Issues

### Critical (Must Fix)

**C-1. C2 ships the shifted tag it cannot confirm on a wholesale-shifted MP3 tag set, on a hold it clears (a wrong title with no flag).**
`packages/llama/src/llama/lossless_titles.py:141-149` (the show-level skip inspects LOSSLESS tags only) and `:199-205` (C2 per position).
Real case, fresh-get view, `darkstarorchestra-2014-07-31` (packaged; `holds_delta`: base newly-held, candidate clean-changed; hand check `some-wrong-not-shift`): the MP3 tags are shifted one early from track 3 on; the FLAC tags and the taper's 21-entry list are right. C2 corrects tracks 4-20 and C1 fills 21 (all 18 judged right), which clears the `unresolved track titles` hold. Track 3 keeps its MP3 tag `Duprees Diamond Blues`, because the reference at 3 is the taper's `?` so no C2 fires there; the shipped tape now has track 3 and track 4 both titled Dupree's Diamond Blues, and nothing flags it: D never runs (no track is left `unresolved`, so check 3 never sees the 17 disagreeing tags), check 2 belongs to D and S only, and `_songish_coverage` is high because 20 titles agree with the canonical. This is the spec's costly outcome on strict surface (a) C2 and on a cleared hold.
Why the spec's own guard misses it: the "two or more lossless tags each equal the reference at ±1" skip is one-directional. The mirror, the DELIVERED tags each agreeing with the reference at ±1 and not at their own position, is the signature of a shifted MP3 set, and it is exactly where C2's yield lives: a census over the C2 notes finds it on 4 shows (`darkstarorchestra-2014-07-31` 17 positions, `lettuce-2002-07-13`, `tedeschitrucksband-2019-02-15` (a swap), `tedeschitrucksband-2020-01-15`). On such a set, every delivered real tag that C2 does not replace (reference `?`, filler, or no agreement) is a shifted tag, not a stale one; `lettuce-2002-07-13` tracks 10 and 16 (`Superfred`, `Headin' Home`, both `tags`, at the run's edges) were not examined by the hand check and are the same exposure.
Fix (needs an operator ruling, it changes a strict surface; both options are deterministic and narrow): in `apply_lossless`, after `ref` is chosen, compute `delivered_shift = [i for i in range(n) if _real(tag_titles[i]) and not agrees(tag_titles[i], ref[i]) and any(agrees(tag_titles[i], ref[j]) for j in (i-1, i+1) if 0 <= j < n)]`. When `len(delivered_shift) >= 2`: **(preferred)** keep C2's confirmed replacements and demote every other delivered real, non-filler tag that does not agree with `ref[i]` to the tag layer's blank (`titles[i] = ""`, one note `lossless titles: track N '<old>' unconfirmed on a shifted tag set`), so `resolve_titles` reads it as `unresolved` and the existing hold, D (which declines a `?` entry per track) or the gated S rung decides it honestly; **(conservative)** skip C for the show, mirroring the lossless skip, which keeps DSO held but forfeits the 18 right corrections on that show and the lettuce/tedeschi fixes. Add `darkstarorchestra-2014-07-31` as a verbatim fixture (description, 21 filenames, the FLAC titles) asserting track 3 does not ship as a duplicate of track 4; re-run `compare` and hand-check the four signature shows' unchanged positions. The 2% bar as computed ("wrong among tracks filled or replaced") cannot see this class, because the wrong track is one C2 did NOT touch; the hand-check brief's "every changed title on the show" is what caught it, and the acceptance report should say so.
