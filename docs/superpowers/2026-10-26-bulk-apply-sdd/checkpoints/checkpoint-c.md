## Release note (llama + emcee 2.20.0)

> **llama 2.20.0 / emcee 2.20.0 — bulk-apply campaign tooling.** Ship together; install both before running any campaign step.
>
> - **Persistent overrules.** `llama fix --overrule`, triage and `--untitled` now record each overruled review flag in `overrides.json` (`overrules`, scoped to the recording, with an optional `note`), so a re-gather or re-brief no longer re-holds a show on a flag already ruled spurious (`overruled: <flag>` in the show's notes).
> - **`awaiting_fix` mark.** Shows the held-backlog fixes would change are marked awaiting fix: a per-show `campaign.json` in the library and an `awaiting_fix` key on the delivered station manifest. `llama status --awaiting-fix` lists them; `llama deliver` refuses a show whose library record is awaiting fix and names the re-process command; `deliver --fixed` re-delivers re-processed shows and clears the station mark; `llama clear-mark` keeps a voicing whose re-processed show is unchanged. emcee shows the `awaiting-fix` state, skips marked packages in `emcee run` and refuses `emcee voice` on them; transmitter does not air them.
> - **`llama reapply` for every state** (held, packaged, briefed, delivered), with `--with-gather-llm`, `--seven-day-ceiling`, `--stop-at-points` and a per-batch snapshot. New outcomes `label-only`, `unchanged`, `skipped: processed`, `skipped: re-selection`.
> - **Strict station audio reuse.** Re-packaging a delivered show copies audio from its station copy (paired by position, title and recording, length-checked) and refuses rather than re-download from archive.org (`station copy does not pair` / `station copy unreadable`); `--allow-download` by name is the escape. `redo`/`fix` now refuse before any LLM spend when the station cannot be read.
> - **Campaign script** `scripts/bulk_apply.py`: `pin`, `arm`, `snapshot`/`seal`/`restore`, `backfill` (records the overnight verdicts' false holds and the Checkpoint A rulings as overrules), `freeze`, `reselect-check`, `mark`/`unmark` (five marking reasons, incl. `newly held by today's checks`), `lists`, `wave-check`, `spot-check`, `revoice-list`, `trial-report`.
