Liner notes

Context

Railroad Earth played Rhythm and Brews in Chattanooga, Tennessee, on November 1, 2008. It was the second-to-last night of an October–November 2008 tour. That run opened in Troy, NY (10/17) and Boston (10/18), went through the Midwest, then to Fayetteville, Austin and Dallas, and reached Halloween at WorkPlay in Birmingham, AL (10/31).

Why this show

The recording is a complete audience tape of the show. It runs about 2 hours 25 minutes over three discs. It was posted to the Internet Archive on November 3, 2008, two days after the concert, and is the only copy of this date there.
